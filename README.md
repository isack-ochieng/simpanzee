#  Good Morning to You

> Yeah, I sent it to her and she blue-ticked me. Dust everywhere. 😂😅💔

What started as a good morning message intended for someone special took an unexpected turn. So, I generalized it into a good morning experience addressed to **no specific person**.

Now, anyone can enjoy it, share it or use it to impress whoever they want. 😂

##  About the Project

**Good Morning to You** is a simple, fun web experience inspired by the timeless art of sending good morning messages. Whether you're trying to make someone's day, spread positive vibes, or shoot your shot, this one's for you.

The original inspiration may have come with a little heartbreak, but the final result is for everyone.
